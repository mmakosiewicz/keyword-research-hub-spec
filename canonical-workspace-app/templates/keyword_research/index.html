🪐 Tiers & Clusters

What this does

Every keyword in your bank gets two semantic labels: a tier (how close it is to what your site is actually about — “Core orbit” down to “Outside”) and a cluster (which topic group it belongs to). These power the Tier / BP / Cluster columns and filters on the Master List, so you can separate on-brand opportunities from topical drift at a glance. Labels are computed here once and stay stable until you regenerate.

1
Core set — you pick the keywords that define what your site is about (your triaged lists, a Rank Tracker project, or a pasted list). Everything else is measured against this center. Garbage core = garbage tiers, so curate it.
2
Embed — every keyword (core + your whole bank) is turned into a vector that captures its meaning, so “backlink checker” and “link audit tool” end up near each other even with zero shared words.
3
Cluster — the core keywords are grouped into topic clusters (k-means), and an LLM names each one (“Link Building Strategies”, “Technical SEO”…). These clusters are your site’s topical map.
4
Tier — every keyword’s distance to its nearest core cluster becomes its tier: ⭐ Core orbit Adjacent Far orbit Outside. Close = squarely your turf; far = topical drift.
5
Business potential (optional) — an LLM judge scores each keyword 0–3 for how naturally your product can be pitched to that searcher. Tier tells you “is this my topic?”, BP tells you “can I sell here?” — the best targets score high on both.
